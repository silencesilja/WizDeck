Looking for your WiZ bulbs

Broadcasting a registration probe on UDP 38899 and unicast-probing the local subnet.

Bulbs are matched by MAC address, not by IP, so a new DHCP lease after a router reboot changes nothing — a bulb that moves to another address is recognised again on the next scan. You can also aim straight at one by typing its address above.
